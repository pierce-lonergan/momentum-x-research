**Today's 0/3 entries probability:**
- Under a 50% true win rate: P(0/3) = 12.5%
- Under a 40% true win rate: P(0/3) = 21.6%
- Under a 30% true win rate: P(0/3) = 34.3%
- Under a 20% true win rate: P(0/3) = 51.2%

A 0/3 outcome is consistent with any true win rate ≤ 35%. It does not by itself reject any hypothesis. But combined with the all-time 2/10 (20%) win rate, the data is consistent with a true win rate around 20% — meaning the system needs avg-win/avg-loss > 4 to be profitable. Today's avg-loss was $93 and avg-win is historically $470 ish (4/24 LIDR + 4/27 OGN). Ratio is ~5x, marginally above breakeven.

**Sequential test pre-commit:**
- After **3 consecutive losing days** (no winning trades on any), freeze sizing at minimum and investigate.
- After **5 consecutive losing days**, halt trading and return to research.
- After **10 trades with 0 wins**, halt trading regardless of session count.

This is intentionally aggressive. The system needs to demonstrate edge, not just survive.
