## §2 — The hard problem, stated precisely

The project has proven, with rare discipline, that **ex-ante selection has no alpha** in the microcap gap universe (docs 245–254) and that **no price-only entry rule pays at any tested horizon** (250–261). Every one of those results is a *point*: a fixed decision time, a fixed feature set. The open question they collectively gesture at but never ask:

> **As a function of elapsed session time t, how much information about the cost-cleared remainder-of-day outcome exists in the consolidated tape up to t — the curve I(t) — and at the first t where I(t) is detectably non-zero (if any), does the remaining move still pay for acting on it — A(t)?** If every rocket is eventually "knowable," there is a race between the *revelation* of the outcome and the *exhaustion* of the move that knowing it would monetize. Either the tape never knows (rockets are martingale excursions and "ride confirmed momentum" is closed for *any* observer, not just our features); or it knows only after payment is gone (the deep structural finding: information arrives behind the move); or there is a window — in which case the project's negative map has a precise, located hole that no prior doc tested.

Why this is more than a feature hunt, and why a smart skeptic should still care after docs 245–261: (1) it is a **curve, not a point** — one experiment bounds every "enter later with confirmation" proposal at once; (2) the null is made **quantitative** by planted-signal power calibration — "information ≥ MDE absent," not "we didn't find any" — which retroactively grades every prior null in the program; (3) the economics overlay uses the project's **live-measured** execution truth (doc-265 fill study, B1 fidelity matrix), converting "is there signal" into "could any tape-reading observer be *paid*"; (4) it runs on a **certified-null universe** — a falsification asset most research programs never possess; (5) per the deep-research sweep, the composite (t-gridded discriminator MI bounds + planted MDE + cost-adjusted remaining-move race) has no direct prior art — price-discovery literature measures *where* and *how fast* discovery happens, not the decision-theoretic race between knowability and payability in a certified-null universe.

## §3 — PRE-REGISTRATION (frozen before running; procedure = `scripts/knowability_frontier_doc273.py`, committed with this section)

**Universe (inherited verbatim, doc-235 ex-ante gate):** gap ≥ 8% at the 9:30 open vs prior close · open ∈ [$0.50, $20] · trailing-20d ADV ≥ $1M — restricted to the tick-warehouse window **2025-08-01..2026-04-30**: 3,553 ticker-days, 134 sessions, median 20 names/day. No additional filters; halts and deaths stay in.

**Data:** `trades_v1_parquet` per-ticker-day partitions (all `data_*.parquet` files). **ET derived from raw `sip_timestamp` only** (ns UTC → America/New_York) — never `ts_et` (the doc-242-era tz lesson, honored at the design level).

**Decision grid:** t ∈ {9:35, 9:40, 9:45, 9:50, 10:00, 10:15, 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00} ET — 13 points.

**Features (38, frozen in `FEATURES`):** computed from prints **strictly before t** — premarket block (6), RTH path/VWAP/volume/tape (24), gap/halt structure (6: gap counts ≥60s/≥300s, max-gap, staleness, return-across-gap — **halts are features, never exclusions**; excluding halted names would be survivorship-of-the-boring), ex-ante context (2: log open price, log ADV20). No calendar features (regime leakage). No panel-derived 9:50 features (look-ahead at t < 9:50).

**Executable label (primary):** entry = size-weighted price of prints in **[t+5s, t+300s)** (≥3 prints and ≥500 shares, else **dropout at t — counted and reported**; a decision during a halt fills at the resume, which is exactly what a real observer faces); exit = VWAP of prints in [15:50, 16:00) (fallback: last print ≥ 15:00). **y_t = 1[gross return > θ], θ = 1.0%** — the doc-250 microcap round-trip standard; consistent with doc-265's measured 94% decision-price fill. Sensitivity: θ ∈ {0.5%, 1.5%} (descriptive; verdict binds to 1.0%).

**I(t) — information:** out-of-fold log-loss reduction (nats/row) vs the train-fold base rate — the difference-of-entropies primitive the MI-estimation literature recommends over MINE/DV; reported as an **estimate with permutation-calibrated inference, not a certified bound**. Predicted probabilities clipped to [1e-4, 1−1e-4] (bounded per-row loss; declared, not tuned). **No class weighting** — the one deliberate deviation from doc-242 hyperparameters, declared here (weighting distorts the probabilities the metric is computed on). Two frozen families: HistGBM (max_iter=300, lr=0.05, depth=4, L2=1.0 — doc-242 continuity) and rank-Gaussian logistic (C=1.0). CV = 6 contiguous day-blocks. **Statistic S(t) = max over families; S\* = max over t.**

**Inference:** **within-day label permutation** (labels shuffled among same-day corpus names; the day factor — market direction, regime — is preserved *exactly*, so the test isolates ticker-specific tape information, which is the only tradable kind for a cross-sectional selector). B = 100 reps through the **full pipeline including the max** (Westfall–Young single-step maxT; per-t significance = exceedance of the null-S\* 95th percentile). p(S\*) = (1 + #{S\*₀ ≥ S\*}) / (B+1).

**M(t) — oracle frontier:** base rate of y_t; mean/median net payoff (gross − θ) of the true-label class, winsorized 1/99 — what perfect knowledge at t would earn per trade.

**A(t) — achieved economics:** mean net return of the **top decile by GBM out-of-fold score** (GBM pre-specified as the primary family — no pick-the-winner), winsorized 1/99, **day-block bootstrap** 95% CI (1000 reps). Raw (unwinsorized) reported alongside.

**MDE(t) — power (the part that makes a null a finding):** plant signal of known strength into the labels — with probability s ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}, replace y with 1[within-day rank of `ofi_last10` ≥ median] — at t ∈ {9:40, 10:00, 11:30, 14:00}, 5 reps each, full pipeline. **MDE = smallest s whose median recovery exceeds the permutation threshold.** Oracle recovery (a detector that knows the driver) reported as the ceiling.

**Pre-named confounds and their handling:** (i) *bid-ask-bounce label coupling* (last-prefix-print at the ask mechanically depresses measured entry-to-exit return → spurious "information" in uptick-ish features): diluted by the 5-minute VWAP entry window; if V-RACE/V-WINDOW triggers, the loading direction is inspected and a mid-range-entry robustness re-run is mandatory before the verdict stands. (ii) *halt survivorship*: halts are features; dropouts are counted per t and the claim binds to the entered population, stated. (iii) *regime composition*: split-half curves (2025-08..12 vs 2026-01..04) reported; a V-NULL additionally requires no half shows a stable significant window. (iv) *day-factor leakage*: killed by design (within-day permutation). (v) *multiple testing*: killed by maxT.

**Verdicts (frozen):**
- **V-NULL** — p(S\*) ≥ 0.05: *no detectable tape information anywhere on the grid*, qualified by MDE(t). Meaning: within measured power, **no observer computing functions of the consolidated tape can know, at any point in the session, which of today's gappers will pay by the close net of costs** — the variance-is-the-product thesis becomes a quantitative theorem; every future "confirmation entry" proposal is answered by pointing at the curve.
- **V-RACE** — S\* significant, but at every significant t the A(t) CI includes/falls below 0: **the information arrives only after the move it would predict has been spent.** The deep structural finding: the market tells you only once it is done paying. Program implication: intraday long alpha in this universe is closed for non-HFT observers *as a class*, not feature-by-feature.
- **V-WINDOW** — some significant t has A(t) CI > 0: a **located hypothesis** (the negative map has a precise hole no prior doc tested). Explicitly NOT an edge claim: doc-234 hostile gauntlet required next session before any further word.
- **V-UNPOWERED** — plants fail at s=0.20: no conclusion; reported as a frontier failure with the reason (legitimate under charter §5.5).

**What is NOT claimed under any outcome:** anything about modalities not instrumented (L2 book, news text, social), anything outside the entered population at each t, anything outside 2025-08..2026-04, and — under V-WINDOW — anything tradable.
