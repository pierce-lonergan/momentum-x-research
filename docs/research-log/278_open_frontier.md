## §2 — The two adversarial stages before freeze (both changed the design)

**Novelty sweep** (`wf_fa2cdd44`): **NOVEL-WITH-CAVEATS.** Nearest neighbor — **Goes 2023, "New Data, New Results?"** (re-runs studies on alternative data vintages, decomposing conclusion-flips into *coverage-flip* vs *value-flip* by holding the sample fixed). Also multiverse/specification-curve (Steegen 2016; Simonsohn) and Minimum-Specification-Perturbation / Fragility-Index (arXiv 2605.01579). Imports adopted: the **decision-flip rate with a denominator**; a **Fragility Index** (distance-to-threshold of non-flips); **point-in-time as-of discipline** (the corrected ADV window must not bridge with backfilled bars — the same defect inverted); and the **inert-because-illiquid confound** (a low flip rate is only "robust" if the near-threshold band is populated). Genuinely new: measuring the **decision-flip rate of a whole research program's universe gates** under a single toggled data-completeness fact, with the inert-vs-fragile reading.

**Design-stress panel** (`wf_d5374dc4`): **FREEZE-WITH-FIXES**, with four *verified* headline-invalidating flaws (the panel queried the warehouse itself): (1) minute-rolled volume runs 10–34% *below* day_aggs, biased in the same direction as the $1M gate; (2) no single minute-roll matches day_aggs on both close and volume (extended-hours vs regular-session); (3) the flip statistic conflates "became computable" (39% of flagged days had a NULL ADV) with "computed wrong" — ADV has three states (NULL / partial 1–19 rows / full 20), not two; (4) **the inert cell is empty by construction** — the research corpus is 100% post-gate (0 price/gap violators), so flips are unidirectional and the raw flip rate is a pure function of the unknown bridge-inflation *k*, hence unfalsifiable. It prescribed: an R-INSTRUMENT-UNFIT escape, `min_periods=20`, a coverage-vs-value split, a Fragility Index, and — critically — **a real ground truth, not day_aggs**.

**The tape tie-breaker (resolving the panel's central objection — and a 277 lesson recursing).** The panel's flaw #1 assumed day_aggs is ground truth. The contamination premise (274/276) is that day_aggs ADV is *inflated*. So I consulted the raw consolidated tape (`trades_v1`, which covers Q1) as the arbiter the panel's "reconcile-to-day_aggs" never used. Result: **minute tracks the tape within ~2–10% both in the hole (0.99–1.10) and post-hole (median 1.05), while day_aggs diverges from the tape by 0.79×–1.92×** (AAPL day_aggs dollar-volume is 92% *above* the true tape). day_aggs' `volume × close` is the erratic proxy; the minute rebuild is faithful. **The verification target the panel demanded was itself unreliable — only a third independent source settled it** (the 277 "verify the verification" lesson, one level deeper).

**Estimand + frozen decision tree (multiplicity: coverage and value reported separately).**

## §4 — RESULT (the enabler works; the fragility is larger and multi-channel than my first headline)

**§4.1 — The enabler is built and tape-validated.** `day_aggs_q1_rebuild.parquet`: **621,164 ticker-days over 54 sessions, all strictly before 2026-03-23, zero duplicate (ticker, session_date)** — verified exactly by the independent cross-tier peer. Validated against the raw consolidated tape (not day_aggs): minute-rolled dollar-volume tracks the tape within ~2–10% in the hole. **task_4994579a is buildable read-only from local data** — the lock is opened. At scale (478-ticker cross-check) day_aggs' `volume × close` deviates from the true tape by >25% on 9.5% of days (ratio 0.77–3.11), confirming it is an erratic proxy unfit as the reconciliation ground truth — the panel's demanded verification target was itself unreliable, and only the tape settled it (the 277 "verify the verification" lesson, recursing onto the warehouse).

**§4.2 — The fragility, corrected by the hostile retest (registered→final distance shown).** Of 1,636 hole-adjacent corpus ticker-days, **996 (60.9%) are hole-affected.** My first headline reported the effect as almost entirely *coverage* (954 uncomputable→computable, **0** value-flips) with a reassuring live-system retroactive-ejection of **8/511 = 1.57%**. The hostile retest refuted two planks of that framing at full strength (the peer confirmed every number reproduces, then the retester showed what the numbers *mean*):
- **The "0 value-flips" was a definitional tautology.** My "affected" set was defined as exactly the rows where the strict day_aggs ADV is *uncomputable*, so the "computable-but-wrong" subset was empty by construction — it could not have returned anything but 0. Running the value test properly over all 640 corpus days where *both* full-20 ADVs are computable finds **7 genuine gate flips** (day_aggs ≥ $1M but tape-faithful < $1M: DERM, PRTH, MPL, RMNI, LFVN, SIF, NAMM), with day_aggs-vs-tape relative divergence >25% on 53 days. Value-channel contamination exists; my strict framing hid it.
- **The reassuring 1.57% rests on a self-selected denominator.** The 511-admitted slice excludes 444 affected days (390 with no measurable partial-window gate basis, 54 with partial ADV < $1M) — 45% of the affected set, structurally invisible to the live-ejection test. On the denominator-honest set (all 954 affected days with a corrected ADV), **78 (8.18%) have a corrected full-20 ADV below the $1M gate** — ~5× the headline.
- **The severe tail is real but mislabeled.** k median 1.04 / p90 1.6 / max 25.6 reproduce, but the k>5 names are **UGRO / FEED / VSA**, clustered at the hole edge (03-24…27), and the max (UGRO, k=25.6) is a *single-bar partial-window* artifact (n_win=1), not a proxy divergence. **"GLXG-class" is a false brand** — GLXG appears once in the corpus and is absent from the tail. The doc-274 GLXG contamination is not what this tail is.

**Corrected verdict: R-TAIL, but bigger and multi-channel.** The universe is **robust in the median** (k median 1.04; most affected days barely move; the effect is coverage-dominated in counts) but carries a **real, ~8% value-channel fragility** across *two* mechanisms — live partial-window inflation (8 ejections) *and* full-20 proxy error (7 more gate flips) = 15 distinct value-channel gate errors — with a hole-edge partial-window tail (UGRO/FEED/VSA), not the GLXG story. It validates 273's "direction-preserving illiquid zombies" *in aggregate* while sharpening the real minority upward from 1.6% to ~8%.

## §6 — Implications and standing rules

1. **The lock is open — and it was cheaper than five nights assumed.** The day_aggs Q1 hole is reconstructable read-only from local minute bars (tape-validated). Questions now askable that weren't: *for any 235–273 result, does it survive recomputation on the hole-filled corpus?* The enabler + the frozen `_doc278_fragility2.py` harness make the whole "contamination-robust vs artifact" catalog a mechanical sweep. (Promotion to the live warehouse remains Pierce's call — the artifact is parallel.)
2. **Your ground truth can be the contaminated thing.** The panel demanded the rebuild reconcile to day_aggs; day_aggs was itself the erratic proxy (>25% off the tape on ~10% of days). When two local sources disagree, a *third* independent source (here, the raw tape) is the arbiter — never assume the incumbent table is truth. This is the 277 "verify the verification" rule, now a warehouse rule.
3. **Define your "affected" set independently of your outcome.** My "0 value-flips" was a tautology because the affected filter and the value-comparable filter were the same predicate. Pre-register the population by a criterion orthogonal to the statistic, or the null is manufactured. (277's scorer bug and this are the same disease: the instrument's own definitions must be adversarially audited.)
4. **Report the denominator, and the denominator-honest rate.** A reassuring 1.57% became a real ~8% once the 45% of affected days invisible to the live-ejection slice were counted. Survivorship in the denominator is the quiet way a fragility gets understated.
5. **The universe is robust-in-the-median, fragile-in-a-minority** to the data-completeness hole — so the lineage's prior nulls are *mostly* safe (direction-preserving), but ~8% of hole-affected admitted days carry a real gate error across two channels; any 235–273 result that leaned on the affected minority must be re-checked on the rebuilt corpus, not assumed.
