# The research log

300 documents, written in chronological order as the work happened. Each one records
what was attempted, what was measured, and what the measurement licensed concluding — including, often,
that an earlier document was wrong.

This is an **append-mostly log, not a manual**. Later documents routinely refute earlier ones. If you
want the current position rather than the history, read [`../TARGET.md`](../TARGET.md) and
[`../ATTEMPTS_LEDGER.md`](../ATTEMPTS_LEDGER.md) instead — those two are maintained as the sources of
truth and everything here is subordinate to them.

## How the numbering works

**There is no document 298.** The numbers run 00–297 and then 299–300. The closures attributed
to "doc 298" in [`../ATTEMPTS_LEDGER.md`](../ATTEMPTS_LEDGER.md) — passive liquidity
provision, single-name option short-volatility, and the LETF close-window harvest — were
made during that work cycle and recorded directly in the ledger; the narrative write-up was
never produced. Fourteen citations across the repository point at it. The ledger entries
themselves carry the measurements, so nothing is lost, but a reader looking for the document
should stop looking.


Documents 00–174 cover build-out, instrumentation, and early results; 175–300 cover the falsification
arc, where the program turned its instruments on its own conclusions. The split is historical rather
than principled — the discipline tightened continuously, usually immediately after something went wrong.

Several documents carry a `_PROCUREMENT`, `_PREREG`, or similar suffix: those are frozen artifacts
(specifications hashed before execution) rather than narrative, and they are deliberately preserved
unedited so that what was frozen can be compared against what was run. Document 296 exists because
that comparison caught a discrepancy.

## A note on tone

These were written for an audience of one, as working documents, and they read that way: blunt,
self-critical, and occasionally exasperated. They have not been smoothed over for publication, because
the corrections are the substance. Where a document says a previous claim was wrong, that is the record
working as intended.
