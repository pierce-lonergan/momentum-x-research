## §2 — The hard problem, stated precisely

The doc-272 substrate exists to run a hundred concurrent experiments; 274 proved its grading pipeline can be fooled by inputs (contamination) and by hidden features (leaks) and that contamination silently corrupts the power instrument. What no one — here or, per the sweep, anywhere — has measured: **how often a research factory fools itself by selection over correlated experiments on real data, and what a dependence-exact family gate costs in power to prevent it.** The experiments share days and universe; their statistics are strongly correlated; Bonferroni is wrong in the conservative direction and per-experiment testing is wrong in the fatal one. Tonight builds the gate — ONE shared set of within-day donor-bundle permutations applied to every fleet member, family threshold = the studentized max over members (White's-RC architecture with permutation semantics) — and *certifies it the way genomics certifies an FDR method and nobody certifies a backtest gate*: a truth-null calibration arm, a residue-detection arm with pre-registered per-t predictions, two crossed dose-response ladders (information-plants and money-leaks) with ≥25 replicate injections per rung, a placebo-decomposed contamination-cost arm, and the D-REPRO organ graded on held-out mutant classes. Why harder than 274: the object is now the *inference layer itself* under dependence; the null is measurably not null and the design must carry that honestly; permutation exactness with fitted members, statistic heterogeneity, plant/threshold interaction, and substrate identity each have failure modes the panel demonstrated live; and a wrong certificate here poisons every future fleet discovery at scale.
