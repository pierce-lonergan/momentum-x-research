## §5 — VERDICT and the L5 hostile retest

**VERDICT: R-INSTRUMENT-BOUND** — unambiguously on two independent axes (Step 1 sensitivity 0.67 < 0.75; Step 1b adjudication 4/6 < 5/6), with Step 0 specificity resolving to "the controls carry genuine latent defects" rather than pure instrument noise. **R-CONVERGES-IN-CLASS is not supported; no standing verdict was overturned.** This is the disclosed modal-expected cell — but reached, not assumed, and richly characterized.

**The scientific answer — does recursive self-audit converge?** **No — and it fails in a measured, structured way. There is no clean fixed point.** Every level, *including the double-verified controls*, yields further verified defects under a fresh adversarial pass, while the auditing instrument itself carries class-structured blind spots (67% sensitivity, blind to OMIT and S1-recompute), an imperfect adjudicator (4/6 golden by strict resolution; a proven-potent seed split), and a falsified clean floor. This is the empirical analogue of **Milawa**: the tower terminates at a *human-auditable trusted base*, never at zero-trust self-certification — and this experiment *locates and quantifies that base's imperfection*.

**The verdict-level error bar (the primary deliverable).** The trust the three standing verdicts (273/274/275) deserve is bounded strictly in (0, 1):
- **Not overturned.** No verified S1 flipped any standing L0 verdict; the one confirmed pressure-leg wound only reinforced doc-275's null result; and the wound arm proved the instrument *would* catch a verdict-flipper on a pressure leg if one existed (2/2).
- **Not certified.** The instrument certifying them has ~67% sensitivity, imperfect adjudicator resolution, and a falsified clean floor. So "these verdicts are clean" cannot be asserted — only "these verdicts were not overturned by an instrument of *measured, bounded* reliability."

That bounded reliability — sensitivity ≈ 0.67 (class-structured), adjudicator directional-accuracy 6/6 but resolution 4/6, specificity leaking genuine latents — **is** the error bar. The recursion does not certify itself clean; it hands up a verdict with a quantified asterisk.

**§5.1 — The recursive tower, one level deeper.** 273 found the blind class in the **inputs** (universe membership); 274 in the **measurement apparatus** (irreproducible statistic); 275 in the **verdict layer** (non-exhaustive taxonomy, powerless acceptance test). 276 measured the **verifier of the verdict layer** and found *it* instrument-bound: it cannot self-certify a level clean, because (a) its controls aren't clean and (b) its own sensitivity/adjudication are imperfect and now quantified. The regress does not terminate in self-certification; it terminates in a trusted base whose imperfection is the output.

**§5.2 — L5 (bounded hostile retest, power-uncertified, disclosed).** The pre-registered L5 was a single self-retest; it was **superseded by the independent cross-model peer review** (§4.1, a different model family) plus the independent dual adjudication (§4.4) — a strictly stronger hostile stage. ## §6 — Implications and standing rules

1. **No clean floor exists for LLM-audited research artifacts.** A fresh adversarial pass finds genuine latent defects even in rulings a prior identical layer certified clean. Do not treat "audited and passed" as "defect-free"; treat it as "no defect found by an instrument of known, bounded sensitivity." Report the sensitivity with the pass.
2. **Audit sensitivity is class-structured; OMIT and recompute-identity defects are the blind spot.** A defect that is an *omission* (a check the ruling never ran) or a value that *equals a stored artifact* is caught far less often (1/3 here) than a wrong number (≈5/7). Any audit gate must be powered *by defect class*, and omission-class coverage requires a dedicated positive-control probe.
3. **The adjudicator is a trusted base, not an oracle — pin its recipes.** Two of six golden splits and one proven-potent seed split traced to (a) recipe under-specification (base-vs-`_clean` artifact ambiguity) and (b) framing. Golden cases must fix the *exact* artifact path and the finding's framing, or the adjudicator's resolution — not its direction — becomes the binding uncertainty.
4. **Plant your calibrated probe on the legs that can fail.** The peer reviewer's decisive catch: an instrument aimed only at non-load-bearing sites cannot certify the load-bearing ones. Any wound/no-wound claim must be backed by a potent plant *on a count≤1 leg* (the wound arm), not extrapolated from off-leg recall.
5. **Echo-scan the TRUE value, not just the planted one.** A plant's original value colliding with a sibling artifact is a catchability tell invisible to a mutated-token-only scan. Scan both.
6. **An independent, different-model peer review is worth more than a same-model self-retest.** The cross-model authenticity pass validated the setup *and* found the load-bearing design bias; the cross-model adjudication is the trusted base whose error bar became the deliverable. Unbiased peer review is the gold standard — and it should be a different model.
