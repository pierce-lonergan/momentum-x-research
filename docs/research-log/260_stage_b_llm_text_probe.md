## TL;DR
Stage A (doc 259) found no numeric base anomaly. Stage B tests the actual doc-259 thesis: does an LLM reading the *soft text* of the earnings 8-K predict forward drift the numbers can't? Built a point-in-time probe (EDGAR 8-K → Qwen3-235B scores expected drift → measure separation), ran it on **400 events, then replicated out-of-sample on a fresh non-overlapping 400.** Result: the LLM-text score is **median-robust, winsor-stable, orthogonal to price & fundamentals, and directionally reproducible** — *better than all 6 false-positives this session* — **but it FAILS the tradable-edge bar**: the long-short median is **not CI-separated from zero** (+2.82% → +1.35% f10 across the two samples, CI crosses 0), the **capturable long-only leg doesn't clear** (the skill is all on the borrow-constrained short side), and "beats the price-reaction baseline" **fails on the OOS sample.** The one reproducible morsel — the LLM is a **weak short-side *fader*-detector** (down-pred ≈ −3.5% vs universe −1.1%, both samples) — is **mechanism-consistent with doc 253 ("dilution is the distribution")**: it's plausibly reading financing/guidance red flags. That is a **risk-filter hypothesis to validate PROSPECTIVELY**, not a confirmed edge. **Track 2 (catalyst handicapping) is feasible (Polygon options reachable) but gated on this** — and this just came back too weak to justify the big build.

## Verdict — no tradable long edge; a weak reproducible short-side risk-filter at most
- **The tradable-edge bar (doc-259 gates) is NOT met.** The long-short median is **not statistically separated from zero** (underpowered, n≈55-93/tranche), the **long-only leg doesn't clear cost** (the entire edge is the short side, which hits the borrow/squeeze wall on small/mid-caps — the same wall as the gapper fade), and the LLM's advantage over the trivial price-reaction baseline **fails out-of-sample.** The earnings-drift form of the information-synthesis game **does not yield a capturable long strategy. Closed as a strategy.**
- **But it is NOT a false positive in the way the prior 6 were.** It passed the median + winsorize gate that killed every prior lead, it's orthogonal to the numeric signals, and the short-side *fader-detection* reproduced in shape across two independent samples. The honest characterization: a **real-but-weak, short-side-concentrated tendency** — the LLM reads earnings disclosures and is modestly better at spotting *losers* than *winners*.
- **Mechanism (coherent, not random):** the fader-detection aligns precisely with doc 253 — *"dilution is the distribution; the rally is sold into."* An LLM reading 8-K financing/guidance language would detect dilution/toxic-financing/weak-guidance red flags → short-side, orthogonal to the income statement, mechanism-consistent. The look-ahead caveat (8-K matched within ±5d of the volume-spike day) means a clean number is **≤** what's shown — a leak can only inflate — so the true effect is no larger than the already-marginal +1.35%.
- **The only forward-validatable morsel:** an **LLM red-flag / fader risk-filter**, tested *prospectively* (immune to look-ahead & lucky-sample) by folding the LLM-text score into the **doc-255 forward collection engine** — measure whether LLM-flagged-weak names underperform out-of-sample, before any capital or live use. This is a *hypothesis to validate*, not a validated filter.
